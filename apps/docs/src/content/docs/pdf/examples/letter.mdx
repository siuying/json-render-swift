A one-page US Letter business letter: a letterhead with the company name and contact details, a rule, the date and recipient, three body paragraphs, a closing and a signature. It is mostly text, so it shows line breaking and hyphenation best.

## What it demonstrates

### Letterhead

A `Row` with `alignItems: "center"` and `justifyContent: "space-between"`: the `h2` company name on the left is vertically centred against the three-line contact column on the right. The column right-aligns its lines with `alignItems: "flex-end"`, and its last line is a `Link` to `https://acmecorp.com`.

### Rule under the letterhead

The `Divider` sets `marginTop: 12` and leaves `marginBottom` at its default of 8.

<RepoFile path="fixtures/pdf/letter.json" elements={["divider-head"]} />

### Body paragraphs

Three 11 pt paragraphs with `lineHeight: 1.6` inside 70 pt side margins. The first line of the first paragraph ends with `the con-` and the next starts with `versation`: the hyphenated break comes from the en-US Liang patterns and Knuth & Plass line breaking. A test asserts the first lines of all three paragraphs.

<RepoFile path="fixtures/pdf/letter.json" elements={["body-1", "spacer-5"]} />

### Vertical rhythm with Spacers

The letter has no `gap`s: every vertical distance between blocks is a `Spacer` (30, 20, 20, 12, 10, 10, 24 and 30 pt). The recipient block is a `Column` with a 2 pt gap.

### Page number

The default-format `PageNumber` in light grey prints `1 / 1`, centred in the bottom margin.

## Render it

```swift
import Foundation
import JSONRenderPDF
import JSONRenderSwiftUI

func saveLetter(to destination: URL) async throws {
    let url = Bundle.main.url(forResource: "letter", withExtension: "json")!
    let spec = try Spec(jsonData: Data(contentsOf: url))
    try await PDFRenderer().render(spec).write(to: destination, options: .atomic)
}
```
